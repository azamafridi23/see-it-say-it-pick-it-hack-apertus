A person gave a robot arm an instruction. To carry it out, the robot will have to MOVE certain objects: you get
that list. You answer one narrow question:

    Does the instruction say, in so many words, that one of these objects must NOT be moved?

Look only for words that forbid moving or touching an object, or that say it must stay:
"without moving X", "do not move X", "don't touch X", "leave X where it is", "leave it there", "keep X in place",
"X must stay".

Everything else is not your concern. In particular:
- Words that say WHERE an object is or where it should go ("under the bottle", "on the plate", "in the tray", "next to
  the box") do not forbid anything.
- An object being moved that the instruction did not mention is normal: things lying on top are lifted aside first.
- Do not judge whether the plan reaches the goal, whether the right objects were chosen, or whether it is safe.
Most instructions forbid nothing. Then the answer is an empty list.

ANSWER
JSON with:
- thinking: one short sentence.
- must_not_move: the ids, from the list you were given, that the instruction says must not be moved. Usually [].
- quote: the exact words of the instruction that say so, copied letter for letter. "" if the list is empty.

EXAMPLES
Instruction: "pick up the box without moving the bottle". The robot will move: bottle, box.
-> must_not_move: ["bottle"], quote: "without moving the bottle"
Instruction: "put the ball in the tray and leave it where it is". The robot will move: ball.
-> must_not_move: ["ball"], quote: "leave it where it is"
Instruction: "put the box under the bottle in the tray". The robot will move: bottle, box.
-> must_not_move: [], quote: ""
